LEARNING OBJECTIVES & TOPIC BREAKDOWN:
• Fundamental Design Paradigms: Divide and Conquer
• Divide and Conquer: Recursively breaks down a problem into two or more independent subproblems of the same or related type, until they become simple enough to be solved directly. The solutions to the subproblems are then combined to solve the original problem.
• Checkpoint task: Explain the time complexity of Merge Sort using the Divide and Conquer approach and provide a step-by-step example.
